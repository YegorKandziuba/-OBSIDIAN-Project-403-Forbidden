# Промты артов — бои и камео

> [!info] О заметке
> Рабочие версии промтов для Gemini: боевые карты, спрайты визуальной новеллы, портреты противников. Фоны локаций — в [[Промты артов — Два дня в Бордо]], стража — в [[Промты артов — Стража Бордо]].

## Что усвоили на практике
- **Одна картинка — одна задача.** Если приложить второй арт «для стиля», Gemini дорисует с него персонажа в кадр. Для спрайтов прикладывать только арт самого персонажа, а стиль описывать словами.
- **Готовый арт правится, а не перегенерируется.** Одна правка за сообщение надёжнее нескольких.
- **Пропорции Gemini на готовом арте почти не меняет.** Рост и сложение задавать сразу, через масштаб в кадре: стойку, ступеньку, руку человека рядом.
- **Возраст.** Блок стиля стражи старит лицо — для молодых персонажей абзац `Rendering:` заменять на «молодое гладкое лицо».
- **Разрешение задаёт режим Gemini, а не текст промта.** Скачивать оригинал, потом Upscayl `digital-art-4x`.
- **Пурпурный фон** вырезается чисто, но съедает фиолетовое свечение. Для фиолетовых эффектов — фон `#00FF00`.

Общий каркас промта для карты:
```text
Create a new, original top-down battle map for a tabletop RPG. Two images are attached, and each has ONE job:
FIRST IMAGE — layout only. Take from it ONLY the geometry: walls, buildings, water, open ground and cover. Every building keeps its outline and position. Paint everything else completely new. Do not copy its drawing, textures, colours or any logo or signature.
SECOND IMAGE — the art style. Paint the whole map in the style of this painting, as if seen from straight above: painterly brushwork, muted night palette of cold blue-greys, warm amber lamplight as the only warm accent. Do NOT take its camera angle, composition or objects.
VIEW: strict top-down orthographic, like a satellite photo; no facades, no tilt. One grid square is 5 feet.
[описание места]
NEVER DRAW: people, animals, tokens, grid lines, text, logos, a frame.
```

## Спрайты визуальной новеллы
Прикладывать только арт персонажа. Направление: смотрит на того, к кому обращается. Формат 2:3.
```text
Create a visual-novel character sprite of the [man/woman] in the attached image, on a flat solid #FF00FF magenta background.
Keep their identity exactly: [лицо, волосы, одежда].
Painterly digital illustration: soft visible brushwork, muted desaturated colours, gentle warm light, grounded realism. NOT anime, NOT 3D.
They are the ONLY person in the image. No room, no furniture, no floor, no cast shadow.
Pose: [поза], body turned about 30 degrees toward the viewer's [left/right], eyes toward the [left/right] edge. Expression: [эмоция].
Framing: vertical 2:3, full body from head to feet, centred, feet touching the bottom edge, small margin above the head.
Background: a single flat, uniform, solid #FF00FF magenta colour, no gradient. Clean sharp edges. No text, no watermark, no frame.
```
Эмоция — правка готового спрайта:
```text
Use the attached sprite. Keep identical: the character, face, clothing, art style, lighting, the flat #FF00FF background, the canvas size, the facing direction and the feet touching the bottom edge. Change only the pose and expression: [что меняется].
```

Character: Hanako, a nineteen-year-old young woman from a far-eastern land, with East Asian features. Long, straight, warm chestnut-brown hair with side-swept bangs; a hairpin topped with a small yellow five-petalled kerria flower. A crisp white wrap-front top with wide half-length sleeves, a dark sash, a long pleated black hakama with ONE small embroidered yellow kerria flower at the left hip; NO chest guard. Character: Liesel, a lady's maid who is secretly an alchemically grown homunculus. About eighteen, a gentle doll-like face with unnaturally perfect symmetry and porcelain skin. Long, straight, almost white platinum hair falling loose over her shoulders; soft muted crimson-red eyes, glassy and unblinking. A black long-sleeved maid's dress, a white apron with a thin lace edge, a soft rounded lace collar with a small crimson ribbon bow, black gloves. ## Кенотафы и Прореха
Стиль не как у портретов, а как у арта «Теневой массы» (`tokenizer/npc-images/prizrachnaya_tolpa.Avatar.png`): почти монохромный тёмный хоррор, глянцевая чёрная масса, как смола. Токены — в той же рамке, что у Отзвуков.
- **Лица Кенотафов не человеческие:** гладкая безликая голова с пустой чёрной дырой вместо лица, как у Отзвука, или безглазая голова с оскалом, как у Теневого подражателя. Человеческие лица Gemini рисует охотно — запрещать прямо.
- **«Края шевелятся, как страницы»** на картинке превращаются в буквальные листы с почерком. Края лучше описывать пеплом и рвущимися нитями.

**Прореха подвала** *(итоговая версия: арт `portraits/prorekha-podval.webp`, токен `tokens/prorekha-podval.png`)*. Приложить две картинки: арт «Теневой массы» и решётку стока, вырезанную с карты 4А.
```text
Two images are attached, and each has ONE job.
FIRST IMAGE — style and material only: the same dark-fantasy horror painting, near-monochrome black and ash-grey palette, deep chiaroscuro, glossy tar-like black substance with flowing strands. Do not copy its room or composition.
SECOND IMAGE — only the shape of the iron drain grate (a top-down view from a battle map). Redraw that grate in the new picture as seen from eye level. Take nothing else from this image: not its cartoon style, outlines or colours.

Subject: a rift in reality floating in mid-air in a low vaulted brick cellar, at about chest height, well above the floor. It is a HORIZONTAL tear, tilted slightly on a diagonal (one end a little higher than the other), about three times wider than it is tall, like a long ragged gash cut across the air. Inside it is an absolute black void that swallows light. Its edges fray and flake away into ash and thin black strands; the tear does NOT glow.

From the lower lip of the tear the glossy black tar-like mass hangs and drips down in long strands and ropes, pooling on the wet flagstones beneath it. In the mass and peering out of the void are several inhuman heads: smooth, featureless black heads with an empty black hole where the face should be, and one or two eyeless heads with a wide grin of sharp teeth. Small skulls are half-sunk in the dripping mass. NO human faces.

Background: on the floor behind the rift, near the far wall, a large rectangular cast-iron drain grate set flush in the stone floor, with eight long parallel slots, and just beyond it a sloping grille of iron slats over a dark water channel, exactly the shape of the grate in the second image. Dark water glints faintly through the slots.

Light: the only light is a cold pale glow from somewhere above and behind, reflecting on the wet floor and the glossy mass. Everything near the tear loses detail and fades into darkness.

Camera: eye level, a few metres away, the whole tear in view with the grate and some of the vaulted ceiling around it.

Format: square 1:1, the tear centred in the upper half of the frame, the grate visible in the lower half. NOT a glowing portal, NOT a vertical slit, NOT a vortex or galaxy, NOT purple or neon, NOT fire, no paper. No people, no text, no letters, no watermark, no frame.
```
Правки Прорехи, которые довели арт до итога (по одной за сообщение):
```text
Keep everything identical, only open up the centre of the tear: a clear, sharp-edged gash of absolute black void running along its whole length, with the tar mass framing its edges and hanging from its lower lip, so it clearly reads as a hole cut in the air.
```
```text
Keep everything identical, only tilt the whole tear about 15 degrees, its right end higher than the left.
```
```text
Keep everything identical, only make two of the heads larger and catch a little more light: one featureless head with an empty black hole for a face, one eyeless head with a wide grin of sharp teeth.
```

**Кенотафы Прорехи подвала** *(арты `portraits/kenotaf-palimpsest|gost|ten.webp`)*. Приложить только арт «Теневой массы», вертикаль 2:3. Общее начало:
```text
Use the attached image only as the STYLE and MATERIAL reference: dark-fantasy horror painting, near-monochrome black and ash-grey palette, deep chiaroscuro, glossy tar-like black substance with flowing strands and tendrils. Do not copy its room or composition.
Setting: a low vaulted brick cellar with wet flagstones, cold pale light from above and behind, grey mist on the floor.
Format: vertical 2:3, the whole creature in view, its head in the upper third of the frame with dark space around it (it will be cropped into a round token). NO human faces anywhere. No people, no text, no letters, no watermark, no frame.
```

Long arms end in bundles of thin whipping tendrils that reach far ahead of it. Its head is smooth and featureless, with only an empty black hole where a face should be. Across its chest the faint outline of a coat and a person's shoulders shows through the mass, as if it is wearing someone's memory — but with no face. Small skulls are half-sunk in its body. Strands of black drip from it to the floor.`
- **Незримый Гость:** `Creature: an almost invisible presence in the middle of the cellar, visible only as a tall distortion in the air outlined by thin drifting black strands and ash. Around it barrels, crates, broken planks and loose stones hang in mid-air at different heights, as if thrown and frozen. At the centre of the distortion a smooth featureless black head with an empty hole for a face is barely visible, half-dissolved into the air.`
- **Тень без хозяина:** `Creature: a flat, man-sized black shadow peeling itself off the brick wall, still half attached to it, stretched long and thin like a shadow at sunset. It has no body, only depth like a hole in the wall. Its head is eyeless, with a wide grin of sharp pale teeth. Long thin fingers reach across the floor toward the viewer. A dropped lantern lies on the flagstones, and the shadow bends away from its small circle of light.`

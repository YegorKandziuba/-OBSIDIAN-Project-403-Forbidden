## Как пользоваться
1. Приложи в Gemini **референс стиля** — портрет `assets/slomannaya-businka/portraits/guard-1.jpg` (стражница Вертмюра с фонарём). Он задаёт технику и свет, но **не форму**.
2. Вставь подряд: **общий блок стиля** → **блок формы** (для тех, кто в форме) → **строку ранга** → промт конкретного человека. В промте человека уже есть строка `Camera:` — её не убирай.
3. Формат — **4:3**, горизонтальный.
4. Готовые арты складывай в `worlds/sumerechnaya-sfera-riverkel/assets/bordo-strazha/portraits/` с именами из заголовков (`strazhnik-1.png` и т. д.). Токены я нарежу сам в `assets/bordo-strazha/tokens/` в той же рамке, что у стражи Вертмюра.

> [!warning] Частые ошибки Gemini
> - **Тянет зелёный цвет** с референса → допиши `The coat is light woad blue, NOT green.`
> - **Делает фигуру раздутой**: широкий плащ, мешковатый мундир → допиши `Slim, realistic proportions; the uniform is tailored and fitted to the body.`
> - **Ставит позу «на камеру»** → допиши `Candid moment, the person is not posing and does not look at the camera.`
> - **Рисует огромную бляху или полумесяц на портупеях** → допиши `The crescent badge is small, coin-sized, on the left breast only; nothing on the crossbelts except a plain oval plate.`
> - **Мультяшность:** большая голова, розовые щёки, «кукольное» лицо, фигура в полный рост враскоряку → перегенерируй с припиской `Gritty painterly realism, small head, adult proportions, cropped at the waist, three-quarter view, weight on one leg.` Если не помогает — **перенеси строку `Camera:` в начало промта персонажа**: Gemini сильнее слушается того, что стоит первым.
> - **Скатка, ранец** → `No bedroll, no backpack: city patrol duty.`
> - **Лента ранга не видна или похожа на шарф** → `The cloak is thrown back behind both shoulders. The rank sash is a flat pressed satin ribbon across the chest, like an order ribbon, with no knot and no fringe.`
> - **Мундир стал длинным пальто, пропали овсяные жилет и штаны** → `Short-tailed Napoleonic coatee open at the waist, oatmeal waistcoat and breeches clearly visible.`
> - **Лента так и не появляется — дорисуй её правкой.** Приложи удачный арт и отправь отдельно:
>   ```text
>   Edit the attached image. Keep the man, his face, pose, uniform, lighting and background exactly the same. Remove the two white crossbelts. Throw the cloak back behind both shoulders. Add a ceremonial sash across the chest from his right shoulder to his left hip: smooth, flat, pressed pale silver-grey satin about three fingers wide, no wrinkles, no knot, no fringe, its ends joined at the left hip by a small round brass rosette clasp, like an order ribbon. Put the pistol into a small flapped leather holster on his waist belt at the right hip.
>   ```
>   Для офицера — то же, но `pressed yellow satin with a thin brass-thread border` и `gilt rosette clasp`. Правка готового арта у Gemini обычно надёжнее новой генерации.
> - **Полумесяц «чашкой» вверх** → допиши `The crescent's horns point to the upper left, like a tilted letter C opening up-left.`

> [!tip] Когда есть первый удачный стражник — делай остальных по нему
> С голым текстом Gemini каждый раз заново придумывает форму: то портупеи сержанту, то две жёлтые ленты крестом лейтенанту, то уходит в комикс. Надёжнее приложить **уже утверждённый арт стражи** (сейчас — `serzhant-1`) вместо `guard-1.jpg` и писать короткий промт «та же форма и та же манера, другой человек» — см. ## Промт по готовому стражнику
Приложить утверждённый арт (например, `serzhant-1`). Вставить блок, заменив строку ранга и описание человека.
```text
Use the attached image as the reference for BOTH the painting style and the uniform. Create a NEW, DIFFERENT person in the same city-watch uniform, painted in exactly the same gritty painterly technique, palette and lighting — not a cartoon, not a comic, not a flat illustration.

Keep from the reference: the pale washed-out woad-blue short-tailed coatee with dark-blue piping and brass buttons, the oatmeal waistcoat and breeches, the stiff dark-blue beret with a small brass crescent, the small brass crescent badge on the LEFT breast, the dark-blue cloak, the narrow black waist belt.

Rank change: [строка ранга — см. ниже]

New person: [кто, поза, эмоция]
Camera: [ракурс и дистанция]

Horizontal 4:3, one person only, never a full-body shot, face fully in the upper half of the frame. No text, no watermark, no frame.
```
**Строки ранга для этого промта:**
- **Стражник:** `Replace the grey sash with two narrow chalk-white leather crossbelts forming an X, a small plain oval brass plate where they cross, a black cartridge box at the right hip and a hanger at the left hip. No sash, no pistol.`
- **Сержант:** `Keep the rank exactly as in the reference: ONE pale silver-grey satin sash from the right shoulder to the left hip, a pistol in a flapped holster.`
- **Офицер:** `Replace the grey sash with ONE single yellow satin sash, three fingers wide, with a thin brass-thread border, from the right shoulder to the left hip, ending in a small gilt rosette at the hip. It is ONE band only — there is NO second band and NO X across the chest. Add a small gilt gorget at the throat and a rapier on the sword belt; the coat is of finer cloth with longer tails.`

## Общий блок стиля
```text
Use the attached image only as the STYLE reference: match its painterly digital illustration technique, soft brushwork, muted desaturated palette, wet night atmosphere, warm amber lamplight accents and the gritty, grounded realism of the face. Do not copy the character, the clothing, the colours or the composition.

Setting: a damp port city around 1800, the Napoleonic era, early industrial age, autumn. No fantasy armour, no magic glow, no modern objects.

Rendering: gritty, painterly realism like the reference — visible brushwork, weathered skin with pores, stubble, wrinkles and tired eyes, muted skin tones. NOT a cartoon, NOT a mobile-game or chibi style, NOT a smooth 3D render, no rosy doll cheeks, no oversized eyes.

Format: horizontal 4:3 character illustration. One person only, no other people in the frame. Follow the "Camera" line of the character prompt strictly: its angle and distance override everything else. Never a full-body shot: crop at the waist, the hips or the knees as the Camera line says.

Pose: a candid, natural moment caught in the middle of an action — like a still from a film, not a posed portrait. The body is turned and asymmetrical, the weight on one leg, never standing square to the camera with legs apart, never perfectly centred. Realistic adult anatomy and proportions: the head is small relative to the body (about one seventh of the height), long torso, natural arms and hands with five fingers. Slim or average build unless stated otherwise. Weapons sit in their scabbards unless the prompt says they are drawn.

The face must be clearly lit, recognisable and fully inside the upper half of the frame with some empty space around the head (it will be cropped into a round token). Background: a real place in the city, dark and out of focus, with depth.

No text, no letters, no watermark, no frame.
```

По образцу **линейной пехоты наполеоновских войн**: строгий приталенный мундир, перекрещенные портупеи, ранец патронов. Цвета, берет, полумесяц и плащ — из лора *(утверждено)*.
```text
Uniform of the Bordeaux city watch, cut like Napoleonic line infantry of about 1805: a tight, tailored, single-breasted short-tailed coatee of pale, washed-out, slightly uneven woad-blue wool — a soft greyish sky blue, clearly much lighter than the dark cloak; no red anywhere on the uniform — with a high standing collar, short turned-back tails, lapels and cuffs piped in dark blue, a single row of brass buttons; a waistcoat and close-fitting breeches of undyed oatmeal-coloured wool; black leather gaiters buttoned to just below the knee over black shoes. The belts and rank marks are described in the Rank line below — follow it exactly. The watch badge is a SMALL, finely made polished brass crescent moon, about the size of a coin, pinned on the LEFT side of the chest over the heart (the viewer's right). The crescent is turned so that its open, hollow side faces UP and to the LEFT (its two horns point toward the upper left), not straight up. Head: a stiff, structured dark-blue felt military beret, worn level and pulled firmly down — NOT a soft artist's beret — with the same small brass crescent, turned the same way, at the front. A long dark cloak of deep, double-dyed blue hangs from one shoulder or is pushed back off the shoulders, never hiding the uniform. This is city patrol duty, not a field campaign: NO bedroll, NO blanket roll, NO backpack or knapsack, NO canteen. The whole kit is clean, strict and soldierly — a disciplined, serious force, not a costume.
```

**Строка ранга** — одна, после блока формы. **Портупеи описаны только у рядового**: если их положить в общий блок, Gemini рисует их и сержантам, а ленту прячет.
- **Стражник:** `Rank: a private watchman. Two NARROW (two fingers wide), stiff, chalk-white buff-leather crossbelts form an X over the chest — thin leather straps, never wide grey bands: one carries a black leather cartridge box at the right hip, the other the scabbard of a short sword (a heavy straight hanger) at the left hip. The same short-tailed light-blue coatee that stays open at the waist to show the oatmeal waistcoat and oatmeal breeches — NOT a long coat, NOT a cape. NO white crossbelts: a narrow black leather waist belt carries the hanger, a small cartridge pouch and a small period-style flapped leather pistol holster at the right hip with the wooden pistol grip clearly sticking out above the flap. The cloak is thrown back behind both shoulders and hangs down the back, leaving the whole chest open. Across the chest, from the right shoulder to the left hip, runs a ceremonial baldric-style sash: smooth, flat, pressed pale silver-grey satin, about three fingers wide, lying neatly on the coat with no wrinkles, no knot, no fringe, no tassels, its two ends joined at the left hip by a small round brass rosette clasp. Elegant and parade-like, like an order ribbon. The small brass crescent badge stays visible on the left breast.`
- **Лейтенант, капитан, командир, капитан-следователь:** `Rank: an officer. IMPORTANT: there are NO crossbelts, NO X of straps and NO oval belt plate on the chest — the only thing crossing the chest is the yellow rank sash. ## Правки готового арта
> [!tip] Сначала спроси себя, попадёт ли это в токен
> Токен — круг от макушки до середины груди. Пояс, кобура, сумки и то, с какого плеча начинается лента, в него почти не попадают. Правь только то, что видно на лице, берете и верхе груди, — каждая правка Gemini немного «мылит» картинку и может сломать удачное. Сам Gemini плохо зеркалит ленту и при просьбе «убери пистоль в кобуру» часто просто стирает пистоль.

Если арт в целом удачный, лучше не перегенерировать, а поправить его. Приложи арт в Gemini и отправь **одну правку за раз** — так он меньше ломает остальное. Каждая правка начинается с общей строки:
```text
Edit the attached image. Keep everything else exactly the same: the person, face, expression, pose, hands, uniform colours, lighting, background and framing. Change only this:
```
- **Лента идёт не с того плеча** (должна с правого плеча персонажа к левому бедру, то есть на картинке — от верхнего левого края к правому нижнему):
  `Mirror the direction of the rank sash: it must run from his RIGHT shoulder (on the viewer's left) down to his LEFT hip (on the viewer's right), with the small rosette clasp at his left hip.`
- **Пистоль за ремнём, а не в кобуре:**
  `Remove the pistol tucked into the belt. Add a small period-style brown leather holster with a closed flap on the waist belt at his right hip (on the viewer's left), with only the pistol grip showing above the flap.`
- **Лишние сумки на поясе:**
  `Keep only one small black leather cartridge pouch on the belt; remove any other pouches.`
- **Полумесяц на берете или груди повёрнут чашей вверх:**
  `Rotate the small brass crescent badge on the beret (and on the chest) so that its horns point to the upper left, like a tilted letter C opening up-left.`
- **Плащ закрывает ленту:**
  `Throw the cloak back behind both shoulders so the chest and the rank sash are fully visible.`
